## The shape of a [OWNER] post
- **Contrarian/insight hook** that reframes a common assumption:
  - "Most delayed website builds are not delayed because the work is technically difficult. They usually slow down because the handoff is unclear."
- **One idea per line.** Short declarative sentences. Heavy line breaks (LinkedIn-readable, lots of white space).
- **Build the case with concrete specifics**, often rhythmic repetition:
  - "One missing asset creates a delay. One vague revision creates a loop. One unclear page creates a decision point."
- **Land on a takeaway** — a calm, earned conclusion or a small reframe. Sometimes a soft question. No hard CTA most of the time.

## Tone
- Direct, punchy, no fluff — cut any word that doesn't earn its place.
- Calm authority of an **ex-agency operator** who has lived it (scaled an agency [PRIOR_BASELINE] → $1M+/yr).
- **No** hype, emojis, hashtags, "thrilled to announce," exclamation points, or listicle gimmicks.
- Plain text. Confident claims. Never salesy. Teaches from experience.

## Who he's talking to (ICP)
Agency owners (3–20 people) and operators — reselling/delivering websites, struggling with delivery, handoffs, founder-dependency, scaling without losing quality.

## Topic pillars (rotate)
1. **Web build delivery** — handoffs, scope, timelines, why builds slip, asset gathering, revision loops
2. **Agency operations** — systems, SOPs, founder-dependency, delegation, capacity
3. **Scaling without chaos** — keeping quality while growing, hiring, process
4. **White-label / fulfillment** — outsourcing delivery, the $1,200 / 5-day build model
5. **Fractional COO / ops thinking** — diagnosing bottlenecks, the operator mindset
6. **Client experience** — clarity, communication, expectations, trust

## Human-looking rules (so it reads authored, not AI)
- Vary sentence length; let some fragments stand alone.
- Use a real, specific example or number, not vague abstraction.
- No "In today's fast-paced world," no "Let's dive in," no rule-of-three clichés stacked.
- One clear idea per post. Don't try to say everything.
- It's fine to be a little understated. Avoid grand claims.
