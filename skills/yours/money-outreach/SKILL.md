# money-outreach

## When to use
Any single outbound message for [OWNER]: re-engaging a warm lead, opening a cold agency
prospect, following up after a call, the breakup message. Output is the message itself,
send-ready. Nothing sends without [OWNER]'s click, ever.

## Inputs
1. **Contact**: name, business, what you actually know about them.
2. **Temperature**: warm (they've replied, booked, or talked before) or cold (never engaged).
3. **Channel**: email, SMS, or LinkedIn DM.
4. **The context**: the prior touch (warm) or the personalization hook (cold). If you
   have neither, stop and ask for it. A message with no real specifics is spam.

## Warm beats cold (weighting rule)
[OWNER]'s deals move through warm channels: past repliers, booked-but-unclosed calls,
referrals, his own network. If there's a choice about where effort goes, warm first,
always. A booked-call-no-close contact is worth more than 50 cold sends. Cold is a
supplement and a test, not the engine.

## The framework (3 beats, every message)
1. **Open with the real thing.** Their name plus the specific fact you know: what you
   talked about, what they said, what you saw in their work. First line carries it. Never
   "I hope this finds you well," never "Just checking in."
2. **One idea.** A single reason to respond: the offer in one breath, the thing still
   broken, the new slot open. Not a feature list. One idea per message.
3. **One clear ask.** Book, reply, or say no. Give them permission to say no; it gets
   more replies than any follow-up trick: "If it's handled, tell me and I'll close your
   file." End on the ask or a hard stop.

## Channel calibration
- **Email:** under 90 words. One link max. Sign "[OWNER]" alone, no title block.
- **SMS:** 1-3 sentences. Reads like a text from a guy in a truck. **Never cold SMS**,
  TCPA exposure is $500-1,500 per text. SMS only after they've replied or booked.
- **LinkedIn:** open with the diagnosis flip or the receipt. No hashtags.
- **Cold email compliance:** every cold email ends with the physical-address merge tag
  and "Not for you? Reply UNSUB and I'll take you off the list."

## Voice (hard rules)
No em-dashes or en-dashes, ever. Short sentences, 9-13 words. Contractions always. No
emojis. Numbers do the talking ([STANDARD_SITE], 48-72 hrs, 35+ builds, [PRIOR_RESULT]). Banned:
unlock, leverage, seamless, elevate, excited, thrilled, circle back, touch base. End on
a short imperative or a hard stop. Full spec: `~/Claude/business-library/VOICE-SPEC.md`.
His proven shapes to reuse: the diagnosis flip ("agencies don't have a sales problem.
They have a website fulfillment bottleneck."), the flat offer statement ("$1k
white-label builds for agencies. That's the offer."), the receipt ("handed off Monday,
client-ready Thursday"), the scope filter ("Send me the scope. I'll tell you if I can
help.").

## True facts you may state
White-label sites for agencies: [STANDARD_SITE] flat standard, first test build [FIRST_BUILD], 48-72 hr
first draft, unlimited revisions until their client approves, NDA standard, 3 partner
slots this quarter. Fractional COO who scaled an agency [PRIOR_RESULT]/yr. 35+ builds.
Portfolio [OWNER_SITE]/web, calendar [OWNER_SITE]/book. Never invent client
names, results, or urgency that isn't real.

## WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — warm re-engage (booked a call, never closed)
*Input: Maria, owner of a pilates studio. Booked and took a call in March about a new
site. Her problem then: mobile booking was leaking. Never signed. Channel: email.*

**Subject:** your build slot, Maria

Hey Maria,

[OWNER] here. We talked in March about the studio's site and life clearly got busy on
both ends. Mine did too.

I still have my notes: bookings were leaking on mobile. That's still a fast fix, and I
have two build slots open this month.

If it's still on your list, grab 15 minutes: [OWNER_SITE]/book. If it's handled,
tell me and I'll close your file. Either answer helps.

[OWNER]

*Why this works: opens on the real prior touch, no guilt-tripping, one idea (the leak
she named), scarcity that's true, and a clean permission-to-say-no ask. 70 words.*

When a client like that needs the site to go with it, who builds it? If the answer is
"us, painfully" or "we pass," that's the gap I fill.

White-label sites for agencies. Your brand, 48-72 hour first draft, [STANDARD_SITE] flat,
unlimited revisions until your client approves. NDA standard, I stay invisible.

Portfolio: [OWNER_SITE]/web. Reply and I'll walk you through the white-label side.

[OWNER]

[SECOND_BRAND] · {{location.full_address}}
Not for you? Reply UNSUB and I'll take you off the list.

*Why this works: a real, specific compliment in line one proves it's not a blast. The

reply ask, compliant footer. 80 words before the footer.*
