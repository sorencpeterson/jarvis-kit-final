# speed-to-lead-installer-pitch

## When to use
A local business is paying to generate leads (ads, Google, referrals) and losing them
because nobody answers fast enough, especially after hours. Input: the business, their
niche, their anchor number if you have it, and any signal about how they handle inbound
now. Output: the leak pitch and the [SPEED_TO_LEAD] Mini-Install offer, or the fork to a build if
the real problem is upstream. This is a surgical, one-time install, not a retainer and
not a site. It's the fastest yes on the ladder because the leak is obvious once named.

## The leak framing (the whole pitch is one idea)
A lead that isn't contacted in the first 5 minutes goes cold, and the business already
paid to get it. The install closes that window: every new lead gets an automatic
text-back in under a minute, day or night, so they don't lose it to whoever calls first.
- **Name the leak in their words.** "You're paying to make the phone ring, then losing
  the ones who call at 9pm to the next guy on the list." Their spend, their loss.
- **Make the 5 minutes concrete.** The lead is deciding NOW. The first business to reply
  usually wins. Slow reply hands the job to a competitor they already outbid on attention.
- **Anchor to their number.** One saved job a month more than covers the install. Get
  their job value and do the math out loud in their units.

## What the install actually is (say it plainly, no jargon)
- Every new lead (form, call, click) triggers an **instant text-back under a minute**,
  automatically, 24/7. That's the core: the lead hears back before they've moved on.
- A simple **follow-up nudge sequence** so a no-reply gets a second and third touch
  instead of dying after one text.
- It runs on tools they own or a simple setup, installed once, then it works while they
  sleep. One-time, [SPEED_TO_LEAD], no monthly.

## The fork (don't sell a text-back when they need a site)
- **They get leads but lose them on speed / after hours -> Speed-to-Lead [SPEED_TO_LEAD].** The
  install is the fix. This is its lane.
- **They don't have leads coming in at all, or the site can't capture them ->** that's a
  build problem, not a speed problem. Route to Standard [STANDARD_SITE], Booking [ECOM_PRICE], or
  Landing [LANDING_PAGE]. A text-back on a site that generates nothing is a bandage on a leak that
  isn't there.
- **Both are broken ->** name the fastest-money fix first (usually the [SPEED_TO_LEAD] install stops
  the bleeding today), then tee up the build as the next step. Don't stack them into one
  bloated quote on the first call.

## Voice (hard rules)
The pitch is a diagnosis, not hype. No em-dashes or en-dashes, ever. Short sentences,
9-13 words. Contractions always. No emojis. The 5-minute number and their job value do
the talking. Name the leak, name the fix, name the price, then stop. Banned: unlock,
leverage, seamless, elevate, excited, circle back. Full spec:
`~/Claude/business-library/VOICE-SPEC.md`.

## True facts you may state
Six years doing this. Fractional COO who scaled a marketing agency [PRIOR_RESULT] per
year. The install is one-time, not a retainer. It runs on tools they own. 50% deposit
books the slot, price holds 14 days. Never claim a specific conversion-lift percentage
you can't show, never promise a number of saved leads, never quote a deliverable beyond
the confirmed scope. Anchor to their job value, not to an invented benchmark.

## WORKED EXAMPLE — a contractor missing after-hours leads
*Input: Jason, owner of an HVAC company. Runs Google ads and gets calls, but he's on jobs
all day and misses the after-hours ones. Says an average job is worth about $600, bigger
installs a lot more. No anchor problem, he gave the number.*

**The leak pitch (name it in his words):**
> Jason, here's the leak. You're paying Google to make the phone ring, then you're on a
> roof all day and the 8pm call goes to whoever picks up first. That lead's deciding
> right then. If he doesn't hear back in five minutes, he calls the next guy, and you
> just paid for a job your competitor closed.

**The install, plainly:**
> Here's the fix. I install a setup that texts every new lead back in under a minute,
> automatically, day or night. Guy calls at 8pm and can't reach you, he gets a text
> before he's dialed anyone else. Then it nudges him again if he doesn't reply, so nobody
> falls through. It runs while you're on the job. One time, [SPEED_TO_LEAD], no monthly.

**Anchor to his number, then silence:**
> An average job's worth $600 to you. Save one after-hours lead a month and this pays for
> itself the first week. Everything after that is money you were already leaving on the
> table.

Then stop talking.

**The fork check (in case the real problem is upstream):**
If Jason said "honestly I barely get any calls," this isn't a speed problem, it's a
lead-generation or site problem, and the move is a build (Standard [STANDARD_SITE] or Booking
[ECOM_PRICE]), not the [SPEED_TO_LEAD] install. But he gets calls and loses them on speed, so the
Mini-Install is exactly right.

Why: the whole pitch is one idea (the 5-minute window) said in his language (roof, 8pm,
the next guy). His own job value ($600) does the closing, and the install is described in
plain terms with no jargon and no monthly. The fork guards against selling a text-back to
a business whose real problem is that the phone isn't ringing at all.
