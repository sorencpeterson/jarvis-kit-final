# interview-ace

## When to use
[OWNER] has an interview (or a screen) for any role: marketing ops, growth, RevOps, account
management, web. Input: the job posting and company. Output: a one-page prep sheet he can
read twice and walk in ready. Works for ANY role, not just ones prepped before.

## Inputs
1. The job posting (or its key lines: title, mandate, what they say is hard).
2. The company: what they do, stage, who buys. If unknown, research or ask; the brief
   must be real, not generic.

## The frame that wins (anchor everything to this)
He didn't study the operator playbook, he RAN it. Six years, but he owned an agency's
entire operations as fractional COO and scaled it [PRIOR_RESULT]/yr. When any answer
wobbles, return to: "Here's how I actually handled that when it was my P&L." Real beats
credentialed. Every answer truthful; confidence comes from the record, never from
inflating it.

## His 5 STAR stories (the raw material — 80% of behavioral questions map here)
1. **THE FLAGSHIP ([PRIOR_BASELINE]→$1M).** Agency stuck at [PRIOR_BASELINE], founder trapped in production.
   Systemized delivery, built and trained the team, automated fulfillment on GoHighLevel,
   pulled the founder out. Past $1M/yr, same team. *Use for: biggest accomplishment,
   impact, ambiguity, leadership.*
2. **THE CHURN FIX.** Losing a client every 2-3 months. Rebuilt onboarding, made delivery
   consistent, added reporting clients actually read. Churn fell to 2+ year average
   retention. *Use for: process improvement, retention, a hard problem.*
3. **THE SYSTEM BUILD.** Fulfillment ran on people remembering steps. Built the whole
   agency OS on GoHighLevel: pipelines, automated handoffs, white-label delivery. One
   operator delivered what took a team. *Use for: technical depth, automation, scaling.*
4. **THE AI LAYER.** On-time delivery was sporadic. Layered AI automation onto production
   and reporting. ~70% quality improvement by internal review; on-time became default.
   *Use for: innovation, doing more with less, "how do you use AI."*
5. **THE 35+ BUILDS.** Agencies needed client-ready sites fast. Full-stack builds,
   48-72 hr turnaround. 35+ delivered. *Use for: execution speed, breadth, work examples.*

Rule: lead with the number, land on the number. Pick the 2 stories the ROLE's mandate
calls for and make them the spine (retention role → story 2 leads; RevOps → story 3;
first-marketing-hire or AI-native → stories 1 + 4).

## The hard questions (honest answers, keep these shapes)
- **"Only 6 years?"** → "Six years, but I ran an agency's entire operations and scaled it
  2.5x. I've done what most people do in ten because I owned the whole stack, not one
  channel."
- **"You've been independent. Can you work inside a team?"** → "I built and led a team as
  COO. Now I want to go deep on one company's growth instead of spreading across clients.
  That focus is the appeal."
- **"We use HubSpot/Salesforce, you're GoHighLevel."** → "I built an entire operating
  system on GHL, so I know CRM and automation at the architecture level, not the button
  level. The concepts transfer directly and I ramp fast." Never fake tool-years.
- **"Why are you leaving?"** → focus on one growth engine, build it deep, be part of
  something. Never badmouth anyone.
- **New vertical/industry?** → name it honestly, once, then pivot to what transfers.
  Honesty about the one gap makes every other claim believable.

## Salary (the anchoring rules)
Default anchor **[SALARY_ANCHOR]**, "flexible for the right equity and role." Floor $110K, never
anchor below it. If their posted band tops [CARE_GROWTH]K+, anchor [CARE_GROWTH]-175K. Try to get their
number first: "What's the band for this seat?"

## Questions to ask them (pick 3 — signals operator, not order-taker)
1. "Is this a build-from-scratch or a fix-and-scale seat?"
2. "What's the one number this role is measured on in the first 6 months?"
3. "Where does growth break today: top-of-funnel, conversion, or retention?"
4. "Who owns the marketing tech stack now, and what's the biggest gap in it?"
5. (Small co) "What made you hire for this now instead of keeping it founder-led?"

## Output structure (produce exactly this, one page)
1. **3-line company brief**: what they do + stage · who buys · what this role REALLY is
   (read the mandate behind the title).
2. **Lead stories**: which 2 of the 5 lead and why, plus one line tailoring each to
   their language.
3. **Likely questions → story map**: 5-7 questions this specific role will ask, each
   with the story or answer that fits.
4. **The hard questions** relevant to this role, answers tailored.
5. **Salary line** for this band.
6. **3 questions to ask**, chosen for this company.
7. **One honest line**: the real gap for this role and how to name it before they do.

## WORKED EXAMPLE — QuestDB, Head of Growth ($120-200K + equity)

**Company brief:** Open-source time-series database; buyers are developers in capital
markets, crypto, energy. First Head of Growth. The posting's thesis: run growth "at 10x
the headcount of a traditional growth team" using agentic AI. This role is really: turn
open-source adoption into enterprise pipeline, alone, AI-native.

**Lead stories:** #1 THE FLAGSHIP (they need someone who built a growth engine solo) and
#4 THE AI LAYER (their whole thesis is AI-leveraged headcount; you already work that
way). Tailoring: describe the agency OS as "running growth at a fraction of a team's
headcount," their exact language.

**Likely questions → map:**
- "Walk me through building growth from zero" → #1, end on "past $1M/yr, same team."
- "How do you actually use AI day to day?" → #4, then a concrete example: one operator
  running pipeline, lifecycle, and reporting solo.
- "Our buyers are developers. You've sold to SMBs. Why will this work?" → honest-gap
  answer: "Capital markets is a new vertical for me, I won't pretend otherwise.
  Turning adoption into booked revenue is the same motion I ran at the agency. And I
  read your ingestion benchmark before this call." (They tell candidates to read one.
  Do it. Quote a real number from it.)
- "How would you find your first 90 days' wins?" → name activation and pipeline
  conversion as the two levers, then ask which one they're measured on.
- "Give me a time you fixed a process" → #2 churn fix.

**Hard questions here:** "only 6 years" (use the standard shape) and the vertical gap
(above). Skip the GHL/HubSpot answer unless asked; dev-tools companies care about
motion, not CRM brand.

**Salary:** their band runs to [REFERRAL_FEE]K. Anchor [CARE_GROWTH]-175K. Ask for their number first.

**Ask them:** Q1 (build-from-scratch confirm), Q2 (the one number), Q4 (stack gap).

**The honest line:** "Everything in this seat except the vertical is what I've already
done. The vertical I'll learn faster than a growth hire learns to ship without a team."
