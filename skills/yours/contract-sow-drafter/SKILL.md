# contract-sow-drafter

## When to use
A deal at [ECOM_PRICE] or above says yes, or asks for "a contract" (objection bank #45: the
proposal IS the scope, this is the one-page agreement that rides with the deposit
link). Below [ECOM_PRICE] the proposal plus deposit terms usually carries it; draft an SOW
anyway if the client asks or the scope smells creep-prone. Input: the accepted
proposal (tier, price, pages, promises made). Output: the agreement, ready to send
same day. Momentum matters: paper fast, build slot named.

## The seven sections, always this order
1. **Header + the honesty line.** Both parties, date. Then verbatim: "This is a
   plain-English working agreement, not legal advice. Either of us can have a lawyer
   review it before signing." Non-negotiable, every SOW.
2. **The work.** Scope bullets, specific and countable: pages named one by one,
   features named, integrations named. If it isn't listed, section 3 owns it.
3. **Not included (the shield).** The explicit exclusion list. This section is why
   the SOW exists. Standard exclusions: ongoing edits after delivery (that's a care
   plan), ad management, SEO campaigns beyond on-page basics, blog and email content,
   hosting and domain costs, logo design, photography (direction and stock only),
   migrating systems the build only embeds. Add deal-specific ones from the proposal.
4. **Money.** Fixed price, no hourly, ever. 50% deposit books the slot and starts the
   build, remainder on delivery before the site moves to their live domain. Rush
   under 7 days is +50%. Price holds 14 days from the document date. Round-3+
   revisions [CARE_GROWTH]/round, stated here so it's never a surprise.
5. **Timeline + the client-delay rule.** Day-3 working preview, live within 7 days of
   deposit IF intake is complete. The rule, plainly: every day an intake item or an
   approval sits with the client moves delivery by the same number of days. The build
   does not start until the intake list is complete (deliver-a-site SOP, stage 0).
6. **Revisions.** Two rounds included. A round is one consolidated list of changes,
   submitted once. Round 3+ is [CARE_GROWTH]/round. New pages and new features are new scope,
   quoted separately. (White-label first builds swap this section for the trial
   guarantee: unlimited revisions until the agency's client approves.)
7. **Ownership.** On final payment the client owns the site, copy, and design
   outright. Until final payment it lives on [OWNER_COMPANY] staging. Stock photo
   licenses logged and transfer with the site.

## Monthly SKU variant (Ops Partner Lite [OPS_RETAINER]/mo)
Sections hold, with: the month's ONE operational system named in advance (that's the
scope bullet), billing monthly in advance, and deliverables listed per system. The AI
Ops Install ([AI_OPS_PRICE]) SOW pulls its scope from `EXECUTION-PACK/ai-ops-install.md`;
offers.md marks that scope TODO-confirm, so have [OWNER] confirm the bullet list before
this one goes out.

## Hard lines
- Never invent terms the library doesn't have. Cancellation and refund terms are NOT
  in the pricing tree: leave a flagged line for [OWNER] to decide, don't improvise one.
- Every dollar figure comes from the pricing tree. No new numbers, no blended rates.
- Care plans are named as the post-delivery option, never bundled into the build price.

## Voice (hard rules)
No em-dashes or en-dashes, ever. Plain English a client reads in 3 minutes. Short
sentences. No whereas, no heretofore, no party-of-the-first-part. Numbered sections,
bulleted scope. It should read like a competent contractor wrote it, because one did.
Full spec: `~/Claude/business-library/VOICE-SPEC.md`.

## WORKED EXAMPLE: [WHITE_GLOVE] White-Glove SOW
*Input: Kelsey, Glow Theory Aesthetics, accepted the White-Glove proposal. 5 pages,
copy included, Fresha embed, consent-gated gallery, GA4. Deposit link going out today.*

> **Working agreement: Glow Theory Aesthetics website build**
> [OWNER_COMPANY] and Glow Theory Aesthetics. July 7, 2026.
> This is a plain-English working agreement, not legal advice. Either of us can have
> a lawyer review it before signing.
>
> **1. The work.** White-Glove build, [WHITE_GLOVE] fixed.
> - Five pages: Home, Services with price anchors, Weight-Loss Program, About and
>   Team (credentials visible), Contact and Booking.
> - All site copy written for you, claims-aware for a medical practice.
> - Brand direction and photo direction (art direction, shot list, stock selection).
> - Your Fresha booking embedded above the fold. Fresha stays your booking system.
> - Before/after gallery built consent-gated. You supply consented images only.
> - Mobile-first build, GA4 installed, financing logos placed.
>
> **2. Not included.** Logo design. Photography itself (direction and stock only).
> Collecting patient consent for images. Replacing or migrating Fresha. Blog posts,
> email campaigns, ad management, SEO campaigns beyond on-page basics. Hosting and
> domain costs. Ongoing edits after delivery, that's a care plan, offered separately.
> Final medical-claims compliance review, the copy is written claims-aware and the
> practice signs off on it.
>
> **3. Money.** [WHITE_GLOVE] fixed, no hourly billing. [DEPOSIT_EXAMPLE] deposit books your build slot
> and starts the build. [DEPOSIT_EXAMPLE] on delivery, before the site moves to your live
> domain. Rush delivery under 7 days would be +50% and is not booked here. This price
> holds 14 days from the date above.
>
> **4. Timeline.** Working preview on day 3. Live within 7 days of deposit, provided
> the intake list is complete: logo files, any photos you're supplying, consented
> before/after images, Fresha embed link, domain access. The build does not start
> until intake is complete. Every day an intake item or an approval sits on your side
> moves delivery by the same number of days.
>
> **5. Revisions.** Two rounds included. A round is one consolidated list of changes,
> sent once. Round 3 and beyond: [CARE_GROWTH] per round. New pages or new features are new
> scope and get their own quote.
>
> **6. Ownership.** On final payment you own the site, the copy, and the design
> outright. Until final payment the site lives on my staging environment. Stock photo
> licenses are logged and transfer with the site.
>
> **7. If plans change.** [[OWNER] decides: cancellation and refund terms are not in
> the pricing library. Set them before sending, do not leave this bracket in.]
>
> Signed: ____________ (Glow Theory Aesthetics)  ____________ ([OWNER_COMPANY])

Why: every promise traces to the proposal or the medspa build spec, every number to
the pricing tree. Section 2 is doing the real work: consent collection and Fresha
migration are the two asks most likely to creep on a medspa build, and both are named
before they can. Section 7 stays a flag because inventing refund terms is how a
plain-English agreement becomes a plain-English liability.
