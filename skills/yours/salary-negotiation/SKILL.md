# salary-negotiation

## When to use
Any comp conversation: a recruiter screen asks "what are you looking for?", an offer
lands, a counter needs writing, or two offers need playing against each other. Output:
the exact words to say or the email to send.

## [OWNER]'s numbers (hard anchors, never improvise these)
- Default ask: **[SALARY_ANCHOR]**, "flexible for the right equity and role."
- Floor: **$110K**. Never anchor below it, never accept below it without equity that
  genuinely changes the math.
- Band tops [CARE_GROWTH]K+: anchor **[CARE_GROWTH]-175K**.
- Always try to get their number first: "What's the band for this seat?"

## The leverage (why he can negotiate from strength, all true)
1. **He has a business.** [OWNER_COMPANY] bills real money; he is not unemployed and
   not desperate. A walkable BATNA is the whole game; his is real. Never say this as a
   threat, carry it as calm.
2. **The record**: scaled an agency [PRIOR_RESULT]/yr as fractional COO. One operator
   producing team-level output with AI. Hiring him below market is them getting a deal
   at ANY number in the band.
3. **They already decided.** By offer time, they've spent thousands of dollars deciding
   they want HIM. Re-opening the search costs them more than $15K. Nobody rescinds over
   a respectful counter; it signals the exact judgment they're hiring.

## The rules (each one is a script below)
1. **Never name a number first if avoidable.** Deflect once, gracefully. If forced,
   give the anchored range and stop talking.
2. **Never negotiate against yourself.** One number, then silence. Silence is the
   strongest move in the conversation; the first person to fill it concedes.
3. **Never accept on the call.** Enthusiasm yes, signature no: "This is great. Send it
   over and I'll come back within 48 hours."
4. **Counter once, in writing, with a reason.** A number without a rationale is
   haggling; a number with the record behind it is a correction.
5. **Negotiate the package, not just base**: equity, sign-on (the easiest yes in comp),
   review-at-6-months, remote lock-in, title. If base is truly capped, sign-on bridges.
6. **Exploding offer = pressure, name it kindly**: a real offer survives 48 hours.

## The scripts

**Recruiter screen, "what's your expected salary?"**
"I'd rather make sure the fit is right before we anchor on a number. What's the band
for this seat?" *(If pressed:)* "Based on the scope and my track record, I'm looking at
[SALARY_ANCHOR], flexible for the right equity and role." *(Then stop talking.)*

**The offer call (whatever the number is):**
"I'm really glad, this is the team I wanted to land on. Send the full package over and
I'll come back to you within 48 hours." *(Never a yes, never a counter, on the call.)*

**The lowball (at or under his floor):**
"I appreciate it, and I want to be straight with you: that's under where I can land.
I run my own shop today, so the bar for stepping into one company is [SALARY_ANCHOR]. If the
budget genuinely can't reach that, I'd rather we find it in equity or a sign-on than
pretend the number works." *(Honest, warm, immovable.)*

**The exploding offer:**
"I'm taking this seriously, which is exactly why I won't sign it in a corner. If it's
the right offer today, it's the right offer Thursday. I'll have your answer then."

**Two offers:**
Never bluff a fake one. With a real one: "I want to be transparent: I have another
offer at $X. You're my first choice on the work. If you can get to $Y, I'll sign
yours this week." *(One ask, one condition, a real close.)*

## WORKED EXAMPLE — the counter email
*Input: offer at $118K base for a Growth Marketing Manager role posted $105-150K.
Below his ask, above his floor. Counter once, with the reason, plus a sign-on lane.*

Subject: Re: Offer — excited, one adjustment

Hi Rachel,

Thank you, and to be clear up front: I want this role, and I'm ready to close this
week.

One adjustment. The offer is at $118K, and the band you posted runs to [CARE_GROWTH]K. As
fractional COO I scaled an agency from [PRIOR_BASELINE] to over $1M a year, and I run the full
growth stack solo with AI automation doing what usually takes a team. That's the top
half of the band, not the bottom. **[SALARY_TARGET]** and I sign.

If base is capped, I'm open to getting there another way: a sign-on bonus, added
equity, or a written comp review at 6 months against agreed numbers.

Either way, glad to be at this table.

[OWNER]

*Why this works: enthusiasm first (removes rescind fear), one number, the record as
the reason, a face-saving alternate path for a capped budget, and a close date. Under
150 words, no em-dashes, no apology anywhere near the number.*
