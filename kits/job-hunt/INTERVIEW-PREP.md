## YOUR 5 STAR STORIES (memorize these — they answer 80% of behavioral questions)
Each is Situation → Task → Action → Result. Lead with the number, land on the number.

**1. THE FLAGSHIP — scaling [PRIOR_BASELINE]→$1M** (use for: biggest accomplishment, impact, ambiguity)
S: A marketing agency stuck at [PRIOR_BASELINE]/yr, founder trapped in daily production, delivery
inconsistent. T: Grow revenue without just adding headcount. A: Systemized delivery, built
and trained the team, automated fulfillment handoffs on GoHighLevel, pulled the founder out
of production. R: Past $1M/yr. Same team, better systems.

**2. THE CHURN FIX** (use for: process improvement, customer/retention, a hard problem)
S: Losing a client every 2–3 months. T: Stop the leak. A: Rebuilt onboarding, made delivery
consistent, added reporting clients actually read. R: Churn dropped to 2+ year average retention.

**3. THE SYSTEM BUILD** (use for: technical depth, automation, "how do you scale")
S: Fulfillment depended on people remembering steps. T: Make it a machine. A: Built the whole
agency OS on GoHighLevel — pipelines, automated handoffs, white-label delivery end to end.
R: One operator could deliver what used to take a team.

**4. THE AI LAYER** (use for: innovation, doing more with less, "how do you use AI")
S: On-time delivery was sporadic. T: Make it standard without hiring. A: Layered AI automation
onto production and reporting. R: ~70% quality improvement by internal review; on-time became
the default.

**5. THE 35+ BUILDS** (use for: execution speed, breadth, "give an example of your work")
S: Agencies needed sites fast, client-ready. T: Deliver at volume without dropping quality.
A: Full-stack builds, 48–72hr turnaround, handed off client-ready. R: 35+ delivered.

## THE HARD QUESTIONS (and your honest answers)
- **"Why only 6 years for a role like this?"** → "Six years, but I ran an agency's entire
  operations and scaled it 2.5x. I've done the job more people do in ten years because I owned
  the whole stack, not one channel." (Confidence, then the number.)
- **"You've been independent — can you work inside a team/company?"** → "I built and led a team
  as COO. I'm looking to go deep on one company's growth instead of spreading across clients.
  That focus is the appeal." (Turns the 'lone operator' worry into intent.)
- **"You're mostly GoHighLevel — we use HubSpot/Salesforce."** → "I built an entire operating
  system on GHL, so I know CRM and automation at the architecture level, not the button level.
  The concepts transfer directly and I ramp fast." (Never fake years; the transfer answer is strong.)
- **"Why are you leaving the agency / open to a role?"** → truthful: focus on one growth engine,
  build it deep, be part of something. Don't badmouth anything.
- **Salary:** anchor with the intel. You know marketing manager median $125k, and these roles
  post $105–200k. Say: "Based on the scope and my track record, I'm looking at [SALARY_ANCHOR], flexible
  for the right equity and role." For QuestDB (band to $200k), anchor $150–175k. Never anchor
  below $110k. Let them name first if you can ("What's the band for this seat?").

## QUESTIONS TO ASK THEM (ask 3 — it signals operator, not order-taker)
1. "What does the growth engine look like today — is this a build-from-scratch or fix-and-scale seat?"
2. "What's the one number this role is measured on in the first 6 months?"
3. "Where does growth break today — is it top-of-funnel, conversion, or retention?"
4. "Who owns the marketing tech stack now, and what's the biggest gap in it?"
5. (Small co) "What made you decide to hire for this now vs. keep it founder-led?"
