# Cover letter system
_2026-07-05. The old default cover mentioned neither the [PRIOR_BASELINE]→$1M number nor the
automation — the two most differentiating facts you have. These lead now. Rule: the
first two sentences carry the number and the systems angle, before a recruiter skims away._

## THE RULE (applies to every version)
1. First line: the [PRIOR_BASELINE]→$1M number, stated plainly.
2. Second line: what you build (systems/automation), not what you "want."
3. One line proving you read THEIR posting (a real detail from the JD).
4. One line on fit for THIS role's core problem.
5. Close: confident, low-pressure, one clear next step. No "I would be thrilled."
6. Under 150 words. No em-dashes. No "I hope this finds you well," no "I'm excited to."

## PER-ARCHETYPE OPENING HOOKS (swap the first line to match)
Tailor in 30 seconds — keep the rest of Version A, change the opener to the matching hook:

1. **Agency ops/growth:** "I've run the exact motion you're hiring for — I scaled an
   agency [PRIOR_BASELINE]→$1M as its operations lead, so I know the fulfillment-vs-sales squeeze
   from inside."
2. **GHL-ecosystem SaaS:** "I don't just use GoHighLevel, I built an agency's entire
   operating system on it — pipelines, automated handoffs, white-label delivery. I speak
   both agency-owner and automation-builder."
3. **AI automation agency:** "Building AI-automated delivery systems is what I already do
   for myself daily — one operator shipping what usually takes a team."
4. **SMB SaaS growth generalist:** "Small teams need one person who owns the whole funnel.
   That's the full-stack operator profile — I scaled an agency's growth end to end."
5. **Vertical SaaS selling to agencies/local:** "You sell to agency and local-business
   owners — I've BEEN one, and scaled one [PRIOR_BASELINE]→$1M. I know your buyer's real problem."
6. **Marketing-automation consultancy:** "I've built and shipped the automation systems,
   not just studied them — GoHighLevel, AI workflows, the reporting layer."
7. **RevOps:** "I've lived revenue operations from the inside as a fractional COO —
   pipeline, retention, the systems that make the number repeatable."
8. **Local-service/healthcare performance:** "Direct continuity from my current COO seat
   at [PRIOR_EMPLOYER] — same buyer, same verticals, provable in-niche record."
9. **Fractional-exec platform:** "I've run my own shop and scaled a client [PRIOR_BASELINE]→$1M —
   the exact operator story your platform staffs out."
10. **First marketing hire (seed/Series A):** "You need a generalist who builds
    infrastructure from zero, not a specialist who needs a team. I'm that operator."

## A/B tracking (the postmortem's ask)
Send Version A to half, Version B to half of similar roles. The jobs system logs
outcomes — after ~20 applications, check which converted to a reply/interview and
standardize on the winner. Story-led usually wins for ops roles; skills-led for
checklist-style posts. Let the data decide.
