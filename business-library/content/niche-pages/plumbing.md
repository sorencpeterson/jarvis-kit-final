A plumbing emergency doesn't wait for business hours, and neither should your
website. Someone with a burst pipe at 11pm is searching on their phone, half-panicked,

tappable and visible without scrolling, emergency service is stated plainly (not
buried under "residential services"), and the site loads fast enough that a stressed
person on bad wifi doesn't bounce before it renders.
