**Subhead:** Built around the one thing that actually matters: getting booked.

**Body, the niche proof angle:**
For a salon, the website's real job isn't to look pretty. It's to get someone from
"I need a haircut" to a booked appointment slot without a phone call, a DM, or a
"we'll get back to you." Every extra step between a visitor and a booked slot is a
chance to lose them to whoever's site makes it easier.

The sites that convert for salons do three things well: a booking widget that's
actually embedded, not a link to a separate app that feels like leaving the site,
real photos of real work (not stock images of someone else's salon), and service
menus that are easy to scan on a phone, since most bookings start there.

**What's included:**
- Booking/scheduling built into the site itself, not bolted on as an afterthought
- Service menu pages structured for fast scanning, not a giant unformatted list
- Photo-forward design (your actual work is the proof, the site should show it off)
- Working preview you approve before anything goes live

**Why [ECOM_PRICE] and not the [STANDARD_SITE] standard tier:** booking is the business here, not
an add-on. The build includes the scheduling flow itself, which is real
functionality, not just more pages. If you don't need booking built in and just want
an information site, the [STANDARD_SITE] standard tier covers that instead. Worth a quick
conversation about which one actually fits.
