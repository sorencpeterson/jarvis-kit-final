# Objection Column #3: "I can get one for [REFERRAL_FEE] on Fiverr"

Source: objections.md #2. Category: price.

---

"I can get a site for [REFERRAL_FEE] on Fiverr."

You can. You'll get a template with your name typed into it.

A real build starts with a teardown of what's actually costing the current site
customers. Broken forms, missing mobile rendering, dead links, slow images, whatever
the real faults are. Then it gets fixed, specifically, on purpose.

Templates can't see any of that. They don't know what's wrong because they were never
looking at your site to begin with.

[REFERRAL_FEE] buys a look. It doesn't buy a diagnosis.
