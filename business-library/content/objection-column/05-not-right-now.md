# Objection Column #5: "Not right now, maybe next quarter"

Source: objections.md #13. Category: timing.

---

"Not right now, maybe next quarter."

Okay. What changes next quarter?

If the honest answer is nothing, then timing isn't the real objection. Something
else is, and it's worth naming instead of parking behind a date on the calendar.

If the answer is real, budget clears, a slow season ends, a new location opens,
that's useful. Now there's an actual trigger to check back against, instead of a
vague "later" that never quite arrives.

Most "next quarter" objections are honest. The ones that aren't are worth finding
out about now, kindly.
