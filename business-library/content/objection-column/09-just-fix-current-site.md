# Objection Column #9: "Can you just fix my current site instead?"

Source: objections.md #36. Category: DIY, template, scope.

---

"Can you just fix my current site instead?"

Sometimes, yes. If it's salvageable, that's a smaller, honest fix, not a full rebuild.

But sometimes a teardown finds structural problems, not paint problems. A slow site
because of one heavy image is a fix. A slow site because the whole thing was built
on the wrong foundation is a different job, and patching it just delays the real
cost.

The honest answer changes based on what the teardown actually finds, not on what's
cheaper to say.

Fixing paint on a cracked wall wastes the money spent on paint. Worth knowing which
one you're looking at before deciding.
