# Objection Column #7: "Are you AI? Did AI write this?"

Source: objections.md #25. Category: trust and proof.

---

"Are you AI? Did AI write this?"

I use AI in the delivery stack. That's exactly why the turnaround is fast and the
price is what it is.

The judgment isn't automated. The guarantee isn't automated. The person answering
this question isn't automated either.

Being honest about the tools doesn't weaken the pitch, it explains it. A 48-hour
turnaround at this price only makes sense once you know how it's built. Hiding that
would be the actual red flag.
