# Objection Column #10: "Can you guarantee results?"

Source: objections.md #50. Category: process, closing.

---

"Can you guarantee results?"

No, and anyone who says yes to that question is telling you what you want to hear,
not what's true.

What actually gets guaranteed: the build, the timeline, and that nothing goes live
without approval first. Those are inside anyone's control.

Google isn't. Nobody honest guarantees rankings or traffic, because nobody controls
that dial.

What's fair to show instead: the before and after of people who came before you.
Real numbers, real timelines. That's a stronger promise than a guarantee that isn't
really one.
