# Objection Column #2: "That's too expensive"

Source: objections.md #1. Category: price.

---

"That's too expensive."

Compared to what?

One missed job a month costs more than the site does. Ask any owner what an average
job is worth to them, then do the math on how many they're missing because the
website doesn't answer clearly, doesn't load fast, or doesn't make the phone ring.

The site isn't the expense. The missed job is.

Price a website against a competitor's website and every number looks high. Price it
against what it's actually replacing, and the math usually flips fast.
