# Objection Column #8: "What if it doesn't work?"

Source: objections.md #7. Category: price (risk).

---

"What if it doesn't work?"

Define work.

You'll see the working preview on day 3. You approve it before anything goes live.
Nobody's buying a finished product sight unseen and hoping for the best.

Most of the fear behind this question isn't really about the website. It's about
handing money to someone before knowing what you're getting. The fix for that isn't
a bigger promise. It's a process that removes the blind spot entirely.

Risk goes down when the unknowns go down. That's the whole design.
