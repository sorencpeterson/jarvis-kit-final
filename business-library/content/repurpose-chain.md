# Repurpose Chain: One Source, Four Surfaces (C61)

Source: 250-IDEAS-BUSINESS.md C61. Workflow doc, not a content piece itself. One
teardown or insight becomes four pieces of content without four separate writing
sessions.

**Status: this is a documented workflow, not a send.** Each surface still gets
human review before it publishes, especially the email paragraph (goes to the warm
list) and the GBP post (public, tied to the business's own profile).

---

## The chain
```
SOURCE: one teardown finding (real, from a real qa.py run, anonymized)
    |
    v
1. LinkedIn post (the full version)
    - Hook, setup, 3 faults, fix, takeaway. See teardown-post-template.md.
    - This is the anchor piece. Written first, everything else is a cut-down.
    |
    v
2. Email paragraph (the friends-of-the-shop insight slot)
    - Take the LinkedIn post's hook + fault + fix, compress to 2-4 sentences.
    - Drop the "takeaway" line if the email has its own CTA already.
    - Goes into friends-of-the-shop-template.md's insight slot directly.
    |
    v
3. Short script (for a video clip, teardown-post-to-video pipeline)
    - Read the LinkedIn hook line out loud as the opening line of the script.
    - State the fault and fix conversationally, like explaining it to a friend,
      not reading the post verbatim.
    - Under 60 seconds spoken. Publish stays human-gated per the content-factory
      rules (voice clone, human approves before it goes out).
    |
    v
4. GBP post ([OWNER]'s own Google Business Profile, weekly cadence per D65)
    - Shortest version. One sentence: the fault, stated as a tip.
    - Example: "Quick tip: if your contact form doesn't show a confirmation
      message after you submit it, you might be losing leads without knowing it."
```

## The rule that keeps this honest
Every surface traces back to one real, verifiable source. Never invert the chain
(never invent a "fact" for a short-form surface that doesn't exist in the original
long-form piece). If the LinkedIn post wouldn't say it, the 6-second GBP post
shouldn't either.

## Cadence this supports
One teardown finding a week produces: 1 LinkedIn post, 1 email paragraph (folded
into that month's friends-of-the-shop send), 1 short video script, up to 4 GBP posts
(the same finding can be a GBP post the week it's fresh, plus recycled as a
"still true" reminder later if it's evergreen). One source, four surfaces, roughly
one real content-gathering session a week.
