Here's exactly what [STANDARD_SITE] buys, and why it costs what it costs.

The standard site is 3 to 6 pages. Built to answer the three questions every visitor
actually has: what do you do, why should I pick you, how do I contact you. That's
the whole job of a website for most local businesses, and most sites fail at least
one of the three.

What's included: the build, two rounds of revisions, a working preview you approve
before anything goes live, and the site handed off ready to run.

What's not included, on purpose: hosting (that's a [CARE_BASIC]/mo care plan if you want it
handled, or you run it yourself), a third revision round beyond the two included
([CARE_GROWTH] if you need it), anything outside the agreed scope (quoted separately so the
original price stays honest).

Why [STANDARD_SITE] and not [REFERRAL_FEE]: a [REFERRAL_FEE] template doesn't know what's actually wrong with
your current site, because nobody looked. This starts with a teardown, the same
diagnosis that finds broken forms, missing mobile rendering, slow images, dead
links, whatever's actually costing you calls. Then it gets built to fix exactly
that, not a generic layout with your logo dropped in.

Why [STANDARD_SITE] and not [WHITE_GLOVE]: that's the White-Glove tier, full copy, brand direction,
photo direction, and the site. Most businesses don't need that. They need the
[STANDARD_SITE] version done right.

One missed job a month usually costs more than the site does. That's the actual
comparison worth making, not [STANDARD_SITE] against a [REFERRAL_FEE] template.
