## The blunt math
$1M/yr = **$83.3k/mo**. Sites alone never get there:
$83k / [STANDARD_SITE] avg = 69 Standard sites a month. Not a business, a treadmill.
The model only closes with three stacked engines:

| Engine | Unit | Gross/mo at maturity |
|---|---|---|
| Builds (sites) | 8-12/mo × $1,400 avg (mix incl. booking tier) | $11-17k |
| Care MRR | 120 clients × $110 avg | $13k |
| AI Ops Installs | 4/mo × [AI_OPS_PRICE] | $20k |
| Ops Partner Lite retainers | 12 × [ECOM_PRICE] | $30k |
| White-label agency accounts | 3 agencies × ~$4k/mo of orders | $12k |
| **Total at maturity** | | **~$86-92k/mo** |

The compounding engines are **care MRR** and **retainers**: every install and every
site feeds them. Builds are the front door, recurring is the house.

## The weekly operating rhythm (what [OWNER] actually does)
- **Daily (90 min):** the 10-block calls (coach on) + approve queue (replies,
  proposals, follow-ups). That's the whole day-job of the pipeline.
- **Build days:** site factory runbook, ≤2h per Standard once import scripting lands.
- **Monday:** weekly owner number (same report we sell in installs, pointed at us).
- **Sunday:** retro (already automated) + next week's block preview.

## Kill criteria (review monthly, be ruthless)
- A channel that produces $0 for 60 days with real volume gets paused, not polished.
- Care churn >5%/mo = delivery problem, stop selling until fixed.
- If installs outsell sites 3:1 by Sep, rebalance ALL positioning to ops-first.

## Capacity truth
At $25k/mo run-rate solo: ~10 builds + 2 installs + care = ~120 hrs/mo of delivery.
Tight but real with the factory. Past that, the first hire is a $25-35/hr Elementor
builder working the runbook, NOT a salesperson. Sales stays [OWNER] + the machine.
