# Long-Game Policies: R265-272, written before they're needed
_2026-07-03. These are the pre-decisions: written now so future shiny objects bounce
off policy instead of eating a week of thinking._

## R265 · White-label operator community (year-2 asset)
TRIGGER: 5+ active agency partners ordering monthly. Not before.
Shape when triggered: private space (GHL communities or a simple Discord), partners
only, content = build teardowns + rate-card previews + priority slots. The moat is
retention: a partner in the room orders more and churns less. Cost: 2 hrs/month.
Until the trigger: the partner one-pagers + monthly partner line in the owner report
ARE the community, one relationship at a time.

## R266 · Course / info-product trigger (the shiny-object fence)
WRITTEN RULE: no course, no cohort, no paid community until $25k/mo sustained for
two consecutive months from services. A course before that is procrastination with
a landing page. When triggered, the course IS the SOP library + playbooks, recorded.
The content already exists; that's the point of writing everything down.

## R267 · The [OWNER] Method (name the system)
The delivery system gets a name and a one-page diagram: INTAKE GATE -> BUILD ->
CONVERT -> IMPORT (scripted) -> QA (scripted) -> DELIVER + CARE. Call it the
[OWNER] Method in proposals and the one-pager. IP that raises prices costs nothing.
Diagram task: one Lovable prompt, staged in LOVABLE-PROMPTS-V2 follow-up.

## R268 · Acquisition watchlist (buy books of business)
TRIGGER to start looking: care MRR > $3k/mo (proof the care machine retains).
Target profile: solo web shops, owner 55+, 20-60 care/hosting clients at $50-150/mo,
tired. Offer shape: 2.5-3.5x monthly recurring, seller-financed over 12-18 months,
migrate to the factory in 60 days. Watch sources: BizBuySell alerts, local chamber
chatter, the bookkeeper channel (they know who's tired). Keep a list in
store/acquisition_watchlist.md the day the trigger hits.

## R269 · Second brand (TradeSites-style) reservation
DECISION RULE: only if the beachhead niche (one trade × one metro) produces 5 case
studies AND >50% of revenue for a quarter. Then a niche brand is an SEO/positioning
multiplier. Before that it's a second website to feed. Domain can be bought cheap
if a great one appears; nothing else.

## R270 · Equity-for-services policy
DEFAULT: no. Written exceptions require ALL of: (1) cash floor of 60% of rate card,
(2) real vesting paper (not a handshake), (3) a board/advisor seat or information
rights, (4) a business [OWNER] would use himself. Maximum one live equity deal at a
time. Anything else: "I'm flattered, my rate card is how I eat. Here's the lease
option if cash flow is the issue."

## R271 · The annual letter
Every January: one page to himself + the warm list. What the business did (real
numbers), what broke, what's next. The owner-report and quarterly-deck agents
produce the raw material; the letter is his voice on top. Accountability compounds;
so does the list hearing honest numbers once a year.

## R272 · Own the rails (annual dependency audit)
Every January, one question: "what do we rent that we should own?" Current honest
answer (2026): GHL (rented, acceptable: the moat is the automation ON it, portable),
Lovable (rented, mitigated: lovable2elementor converts OUT), ElevenLabs (rented,
mitigated: local say fallback), Claude (rented, mitigated: playbooks + prompts are
ours and model-agnostic), Stripe (rented, standard). The brain, factory, playbooks,
voice, and client relationships are owned. Close one dependency per year or
consciously re-rent it.
