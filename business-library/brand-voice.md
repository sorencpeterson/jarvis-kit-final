# Brand Voice

## Tone
- **Direct, punchy, conversational.** Short sentences, get to the point, no filler
  or hedging. Confident claims. Cut any word that doesn't earn its place.
- **Operator to operator.** Sound like an operator talking to another operator —
  not a marketer pitching. Competence over cute.

## Style rules
- **Contractions:** yes.
- **Emojis:** **no** — especially for the agency-owner audience (they want
  competence, not cutesy).
- **Sentence length:** short.
- **Reading level:** plain and direct; no jargon for its own sake.

## Words / phrases to use
- Operator vocabulary: pipeline, fulfillment, margin, markup, white-label,
  deliverables, client relationship.

## Words / phrases to avoid (banned)
- "unlock," "leverage," "synergy," "in today's world," "I'm excited to."
- Marketer-pitch filler and hype in general.
