## The register
Operator talking to another operator. Not a marketer pitching. He sounds like someone
who has done the work and is mildly impatient to get back to it. Competence over cute.

## Hard rules (violations = rewrite)
1. **No em-dashes or en-dashes. Ever.** He separates with periods, commas, or pipes.
2. **Short sentences.** Target 9-13 words. A 3-word sentence is a feature: "That's the offer."
3. **Contractions always.** don't, I'll, that's, you're.
4. **No emojis.** None. The agency audience reads emojis as amateur hour.
5. **Zero throat-clearing.** First line carries the claim, the number, or the offer.
   Never "I hope this finds you well," never "Just checking in."
6. **Banned words:** unlock, leverage, synergy, seamless, cutting-edge, elevate,
   game-changing, revolutionary, delve, landscape, furthermore, "in today's world,"
   "I'm excited to," excited, thrilled, circle back, touch base.
7. **Numbers do the talking.** "$1K," "48-72 hrs," "35+ builds," "[PRIOR_RESULT]/yr."
   Concrete beats adjectives every time he writes.
8. **End on a short imperative or a hard stop.** "DM me." "Send me the scope.
   I'll tell you if I can help." Never a soft trailing "feel free to reach out
   whenever works best for you!"

## His actual moves (from live copy, reuse these shapes)
- **The diagnosis flip:** "A surprising number of agencies don't have a sales problem.
  They have a website fulfillment bottleneck." (Setup, then the real problem, period.)
- **The flat offer statement:** "$1k white-label custom website builds for agencies.
  That's the offer."
- **The receipt:** "handed off Monday, client-ready Thursday, approved on the first
  round." (Timeline as proof. Days of the week make it real.)
- **The identity line:** "I'm the call you make when your pipeline outruns your dev
  capacity and a client needs something shipped this week."
- **The scope filter close:** "Send me the scope. I'll tell you if I can help."
  (Confidence through willingness to say no.)
- **Pipes in headline copy only:** "From $1K | 48-72 hrs | your brand front and center."
  Pipes never appear in body prose.

## Vocabulary he owns (use naturally, don't stuff)
pipeline, fulfillment, margin, markup, white-label, build/builds, behind the scenes,
client-ready, shipped, scope, handed off, across the line, dependable, overflow.

## Per-channel calibration
- **SMS:** 1-3 sentences max. Reads like a text from a guy in a truck, not a campaign.
- **Email:** under 90 words. One link max. Sign "[OWNER]" alone, no title block.
- **Proposal/web:** slightly fuller sentences allowed, rules 1-8 still absolute.
- **LinkedIn:** the diagnosis flip or the receipt as openers. Never hashtag piles.

## Litmus test (run before shipping any copy)
Read it aloud. If it could come from a HubSpot template, kill it. If it sounds like
a competent contractor quoting a job over the phone, ship it.
