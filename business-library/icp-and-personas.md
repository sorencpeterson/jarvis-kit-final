# ICP & Personas

## Primary ICP
- **Who:** Owners of **small-to-mid digital/marketing agencies, ~3–20 people**.
  They sell websites as part of their service stack but don't want to staff a full
  dev team.
- **Top pains:**
  1. **Delivery pipeline is the bottleneck** — clients wait weeks; the owner is
     stuck doing dev work they hate, or babysitting unreliable offshore contractors.
  2. **Inconsistent quality and missed deadlines** from whoever they use now.
  3. **Leaving money on the table** — turning down web work because they can't
     fulfill it fast enough.
- **Desires / outcomes:** A reliable, fast, **white-label dev partner they can mark
  up and resell** — one who makes them look good and **never touches the client
  relationship**.
- **Objections (the big three, with the counters that work):**
  1. **"Can I trust the quality/speed?"** → Don't argue, de-risk: first test build is
     [FIRST_BUILD] flat, working draft in 48-72 hrs, unlimited revisions until THEIR client
     approves. "If it's not the smoothest build you've had all year, don't send another."
  2. **"Will you go around me to my client?"** → The whole model depends on invisibility.
     NDA standard, their brand on everything, their client never learns [OWNER] exists.
     "You look great, I stay out of it."
  3. **"How's this different from my offshore guy / Fiverr?"** → The COO wedge: "I ran
     ops inside an agency and scaled it [PRIOR_RESULT]. I know what a client breathing
     down your neck feels like. Fiverr can't say that." Reliability sold via the receipt
     (handed off Monday, client-ready Thursday), not adjectives.
- **How they talk:** Operator language — pipeline, fulfillment, margin, markup,
  deliverables, client relationship. Address them **operator-to-operator**, not
  marketer-to-prospect.
