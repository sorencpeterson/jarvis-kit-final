## The spine, one breath
[OWNER_COMPANY] is the white-label build partner that ran agency operations.
Fiverr sells you a template. Offshore sells you hours. I sell you a delivered,
client-ready site under your brand, from someone who scaled an agency from [PRIOR_BASELINE]
to $1M a year from the COO seat. I don't just build. I run the system.

That last sentence is the whole position. Everything below is it, applied.

## The wedge
Every competitor can say fast and cheap. Nobody else can claim the chair.

- **6 years** running growth and ops. Never rounded up
  (`~/Claude/second-brain/LETTER-TO-THE-NEXT-MODELS.md`).
- **Fractional COO inside a marketing agency: [PRIOR_RESULT] per YEAR**
  (`~/Claude/second-brain/store/case/400k-to-1m.html`). Per year, always. A
  per-month version is a known error in circulation; see `proof-receipts-library.md` R1.
- **He's been the buyer.** He babysat the offshore contractors, watched an account
  churn every 2-3 months, and fixed it with systems, not hires (same case study).
  So he knows what a blown deadline actually costs an agency: the client
  relationship, not the invoice.
- **The delivery system has a name and gates:** the [OWNER] Method
  (`[OWNER_HANDLE]-method.md`). Intake gate, scripted pipeline, QA report attached to
  every delivery. Reliability as architecture, not adjectives.

Canonical wedge line (use verbatim or tighter, `icp-and-personas.md`):
"I ran ops inside an agency and scaled it [PRIOR_RESULT] a year. I know what a client
breathing down your neck feels like. Fiverr can't say that."

## We are the X that does Y, unlike Z
The spine per audience. Never lead with price or speed; those are the two axes
Fiverr wins, and the LinkedIn audit showed the wedge is the one row where every
competitor scores lowest (`~/Claude/MARKETING-AUDIT-linkedin.md`, competitor table).

1. **To agencies (core):** We're the white-label build partner that ran agency ops.
   Unlike Fiverr and your offshore guy, I know what losing a client relationship
   costs, because I've sat on your side of it. Your brand on everything, NDA
   standard, first build [FIRST_BUILD] flat to prove it.
2. **To medspa owners:** I'm the fixed-price builder whose sites book patients,
   unlike the $1,500-2,500/mo retainer agency that sends reports
   (`sops/niche-books/medspa.md`). One retained tox patient is ~[PROJECT_EXAMPLE]/yr. Two
   patients pay for the whole site.
3. **To men's-health clinics:** Same anti-retainer spine, tuned for discretion:
   your patients decide at night and won't explain low T to a front desk. I build
   the quiet path from search to booked consult, fixed price, no retainer.
4. **To trades and local service:** I'm the guy who makes the phone ring, priced in
   jobs, not dollars. Unlike the nephew and the [REFERRAL_FEE] template, the build starts
   with a teardown of what's actually costing you customers
   (`playbooks/objections.md` #2, `playbooks/sales-calls.md` ICP A).
5. **To buried founders:** I'm the fractional COO who's operated it, not advised
   it. Unlike a consultant with a binder, I install the system, prove it runs, and
   hand it over. "Build what the founder cannot be" (case study close line).

## What we are NOT (category fences)
- **Not an agency.** No retainers-for-reports. Fixed SKU, fixed scope, fixed
  timeline, never hourly (`playbooks/pricing-tree.md` hard rule 6). The
  anti-retainer stance IS the local-niche pitch.
- **Not a marketplace freelancer.** No bidding, no portfolio roulette. "Send me the
  scope. I'll tell you if I can help" (VOICE-SPEC). Saying no fast is part of the
  product.
- **Not a dev shop selling capacity.** Agencies buy delivered outcomes with their
  name on them, not hours.
- **Not cheap, on purpose.** Price-only buyers should take Fiverr, and we say so
  out loud (`playbooks/objections.md` #34). Confidence beats gatekeeping.

## Message architecture (the proven moves)
From live published copy, catalogued in `VOICE-SPEC.md`. Reuse the shapes:
- **The diagnosis flip:** "A surprising number of agencies don't have a sales
  problem. They have a website fulfillment bottleneck."
- **The flat offer:** "$1k white-label custom website builds for agencies. That's
  the offer."
- **The receipt:** "handed off Monday, client-ready Thursday, approved on the first
  round."
- **The identity line:** "I'm the call you make when your pipeline outruns your dev
  capacity and a client needs something shipped this week."
- **The scope filter:** "Send me the scope. I'll tell you if I can help."

Placement rule: the wedge goes in the headline and the first line, never paragraph
six. Burying it was the single most expensive positioning error found in the
LinkedIn audit.

## Delivery promises, reconciled (say it the same way everywhere)
The 48-72hr vs 5-day mismatch was flagged as a live trust leak
(`~/Claude/MARKETING-AUDIT-linkedin.md`). The canonical reconciliation:
- **White-label builds: 48-72 hours from complete assets.** The intake gate is what
  makes that honest: the clock starts when everything's in hand
  (`sops/deliver-a-site.md` Stage 0, `~/Claude/MONEY-THIS-MONTH.md` §8).
- **Direct clients: 7 days deposit to live, working preview on day 3.** Rush is
  +50% (pricing-tree rules 2 and 7 pre-empts, objections #18).
- **Revisions:** unlimited until the agency's client approves on the white-label
  first build (offers.md guarantee). Direct rate card: two rounds included,
  [CARE_GROWTH]/round after, said upfront (pricing-tree rule 4). Don't mix the two.

## Proof discipline
Every number in outbound copy traces to `proof-receipts-library.md`. If it's not
VERIFIED there, it doesn't ship. Never invent evidence; when proof is thin, say
what was verified (`~/Claude/second-brain/LETTER-TO-THE-NEXT-MODELS.md`, temptation
#2). The one receipt we still lack and want most: a build-specific agency
testimonial ("sent a scope Monday, client-ready Wednesday"). Ask for it at every
delivery.
