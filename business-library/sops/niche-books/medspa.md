# Niche book: MEDSPA / AESTHETICS — the beachhead
_2026-07-03, Fable-depth rewrite. This is now the primary niche: the warm list is
majority medspa/clinic, all 9 magazine proposals are medspa. Every claim here is
either industry-standard economics or observed in OUR OWN teardowns today._

## Why this niche wins for us
1. **High ticket, recurring revenue clients**: a retained aesthetics patient is worth
   $1,500-5,000/yr (tox 3-4x/yr at [CARE_PREMIUM]-600, filler 1-2x at $600-1,200, laser/RF
   packages $1,500-3,000, memberships $99-299/mo). Owners UNDERSTAND paying for
   customer acquisition because one patient pays for the whole site.
2. **Visual business, weak sites**: they sell before/afters and trust, yet our 9
   teardowns found: heroes that bury the offer, 2-6 photos total, no booking above
   the fold, sites that are literally Fresha/Vagaro rental pages. The gap between
   what they sell and what their site shows is our entire pitch.
3. **They already buy marketing**: most have been burned by a $1,500-2,500/mo agency
   retainer that produced reports, not bookings. Our fixed-price build + optional
   care is the anti-retainer positioning.
4. **The GLP-1 goldrush (2025-26)**: weight-loss programs ([CARE_PREMIUM]-500/mo memberships)
   are the fastest-growing revenue line. Owners are actively landing-page hungry for
   it (Lesley already runs a "GLP-1 Bridge Program"). Every proposal should ask
   about it.

## Who signs the check
Owner-injector (NP/RN/MD) or a practice manager. The owner-injector is clinical-first,
suspicious of marketers, allergic to jargon: talk patient volume and no-show rates,
not CTR. The practice manager cares about the phone ringing and the calendar filling.
Decision cycles are FAST (days) when the pain is bookings; slow when it's "brand."

## What their site must do (our build spec, in priority order)
1. **Booking above the fold** — they lose the 9pm impulse booker without it. Integrate
   what they already run: Vagaro, Fresha, Boulevard, Aesthetic Record, Mindbody,
   Jane. NEVER propose replacing the booking system; embed it.
2. **Faces and results**: team credentials (licenses visible — patients vet
   injectors) + before/after gallery WITH signed consent (see compliance).
3. **Service menu with price anchors**: "from $X" beats hidden pricing; hiding prices
   sends patients to the competitor who shows them.
4. **The membership/GLP-1 page**: their recurring-revenue lines get dedicated pages,
   not a paragraph.
5. **Reviews streamed in** (Google, RealSelf where applicable).
6. **Financing logos** (Cherry, CareCredit) — average tickets need them.
7. Mobile-first extremes: this audience books from Instagram on a phone at night.

## Compliance rails (say these; they build trust)
- Before/afters need signed patient consent; we build the gallery, THEY supply
  consented images only.
- No medical outcome claims in copy ("eliminates wrinkles" -> "smooths the look of").
- GLP-1/weight-loss copy stays away from drug brand names in ad-adjacent pages
  (trademark + platform-policy headaches); "medical weight loss program" framing.
- HIPAA-adjacent: no patient data through the site beyond the booking platform they
  already use; forms collect name/contact only, never medical history.

## Seasonality calendar (drives outreach + care-plan content)
- Jan-Apr: laser/resurfacing season (downtime hides indoors), "summer body" GLP-1 push.
- May-Jul: tox/filler before events; bridal.
- Sep-Oct: post-summer pigment correction.
- Nov-Dec: gift cards + memberships (the gift-card page pays for itself).

## Their objections (observed + expected) and our counters
1. "All my clients come from Instagram" -> "Instagram finds them, your site loses
   them. They tap your bio link and land on a Fresha page with no faces, no prices,
   no proof. You're paying for the click and dropping the catch."
2. "I have a marketing agency" -> "Keep them for ads. I fix where the ads LAND. Ask
   them what your booking conversion is; if they can't answer, that's the problem."
3. "Compliance makes websites risky" -> "That's why the gallery is consent-gated and
   the copy stays claims-clean. I build medspa sites; I know where the lines are."
4. "We're fully booked" -> "Booked with your best-margin services? A site that sells
   memberships and GLP-1 fills the HIGH-value calendar, not just the busy one."
5. Price -> anchor to patient LTV (above), then the two-patient payback line.

## The wedge questions (discovery, this niche)

2. "Where do bookings come from now, and what happens to someone who finds you at 9pm?"
