## Recommended SKU routing
Per `business-library/playbooks/pricing-tree.md`'s routing rules: **Local
service (HVAC...) → Standard [STANDARD_SITE].** If they take appointments/scheduling →
E-com/Booking [ECOM_PRICE]. **Always attach Care Growth in the proposal.** It
converts at delivery, not at signing, per the upsell path.

## Seasonality (real, drives the Seasonal Tune-Up sell)
HVAC runs on two hard seasonal turns: **spring (AC push)** and **fall
(furnace season)**, explicitly named in `250-IDEAS-BUSINESS.md` A8 as the
seasonal tune-up trigger pattern. This is a real, checkable calendar pattern,
not a guess. See `EXECUTION-PACK/offers/a8-seasonal-tune-up.md` for the
$295 SKU built around exactly this rhythm. Storm-response readiness
(`250-IDEAS-BUSINESS.md` D74) is a roofing/restoration pattern primarily, but
HVAC owners in storm-prone metros feel a related spike after major weather
events (AC/furnace failures compound with power outages).

## Objections most likely on an HVAC call
Straight from `business-library/playbooks/objections.md`, the price
objections hit hardest here because job values are concrete and easy to
anchor to:
- **"That's too expensive."** → "Compared to what? One missed job a month
  costs you more than this site does. What's an average job worth for you?"
- **"I can get a site for [REFERRAL_FEE] on Fiverr."** → contrast the teardown
  diagnosis against a template that "can't see."
- **"Your competitor quoted me $600."** → "Ask them to walk you through five
  specific faults on your current site first."
Full 50-item library covers timing, trust, and scope objections too. Pull
the relevant section before any HVAC call.

## The call script (ICP A: local service owner)
Per `business-library/playbooks/sales-calls.md` ICP A. This is written for
exactly this owner type: "owner-operator, phone in a truck, hates meetings,
respects trades. Talk like a contractor, not a marketer. Price in jobs, not
dollars." The three-question dig ("What's an average job worth to you?" /
"Where do customers come from right now, and what happens when they hit your
site?" / "If the phone rang twice more a week, what would that change?") is
the exact frame to use.
