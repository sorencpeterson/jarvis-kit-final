campaigns lives here with its source and its status. If a number isn't in this
file as VERIFIED, it doesn't ship. The rule behind the rule: never invent
evidence; when proof is thin, say what was verified
(`~/Claude/second-brain/LETTER-TO-THE-NEXT-MODELS.md`, temptation #2). One
fabricated receipt poisons the whole positioning._

**Statuses.**
- **VERIFIED**: traces to a primary source in this workspace (ledger, case study,
  a live-copy audit, a store).
- **PUBLISHED, UNVERIFIED**: [OWNER] has said it publicly (site, resume, LinkedIn),
  but no primary source exists in the workspace. Usable only because he already
  stands behind it publicly; do not amplify further until sourced.
- **PROCESS**: true by construction; the repo or SOP is the proof.
