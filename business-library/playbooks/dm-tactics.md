# DM tactics (agency white-label outreach)

Source: Ty's 10 long-kept DM tactics, distilled into [OWNER]'s context (DMing agency
owners about white-label web builds) and mapped to where each lives in the system.
Voice rules still apply to every drafted message: no em-dashes, punchy, first person,
one clear ask.

Legend: [AUTO] the system already does this or drafts for it. [MANUAL] [OWNER]'s own play,
higher-touch, not automated on purpose. [CONFIG] a knob or timing choice.

## 1. Authoritative profile (the landing page) [AUTO + MANUAL]
The DM sends them to the profile, so the profile closes or kills the call. Post 2-3x a
week (case studies, proof, stories). [AUTO] The authority arc + commenter sourcing cover
posting and reach. [MANUAL] Get 99+ endorsements on the top 2 skills, 2+ client
recommendations, keep stacking social proof. That part is [OWNER]'s to collect.

## 2. The empty (noteless) connection request [AUTO + CONFIG]
Noteless connects out-pull noted ones. [AUTO] Connects already go out noteless (his
standing preference). [CONFIG] Best acceptance is a send at 8-10am in the PROSPECT'S
timezone; the engage run currently fires once at 6pm his time. A per-prospect
morning-send window is the open lever if acceptance dips. Volume ceiling stays 100/week
(LinkedIn's real invite limit), never past it.

## 3. The restrained compliment [AUTO]
Compliment like a peer who has done the work, understated and specific, never gushing.
"Solid case study, 3.2x ROAS is strong for e-com" beats "I LOVE your work." Enthusiasm
reads as desperate; understatement reads as expertise. Baked into the DM opener draft
prompt (li_engager_dm.py, 2026-07-15). Prefer no compliment over an eager one.

## 4. The question attack [AUTO opener / MANUAL live]
2-4 questions at once gets a prospect to open up. The OPENER stays one soft question on
purpose ([OWNER]'s approved shape, cleaner for a first cold-ish touch). Save the 2-3
question stack for the live back-and-forth once they reply.

## 5. The value foreshadower [MANUAL follow-up]
Hint at concrete value: a resource, an intro, a specific result you got. "Just took a
brand from 2x to 4x ROAS in a few weeks" or "have 9 pages of notes on that book, want
them?" Fits the FOLLOW-UP, not the first opener (the opener is question-first, no pitch).
A conveyor/follow-up enhancement to build when wanted.

## 6. The connector [MANUAL, ties to referral SKU]
Offer to connect them with a killer in a space they're weak in (podcast, newsletter,
paid). Win-win-win, and it pairs with the [REFERRAL_FEE] referral-partner SKU. "Know some strong
people in that space, want an intro?"

## 7. The outcome-independent voice message [MANUAL, deliberately not automated]
Voice notes warm people up because they prove a 1-on-1 human, not a blast. Grounded,
value-first tonality, prying into pain points. NOT automated: automated outreach turns
people off, and the DM lane drafts text for [OWNER]'s approval rather than blasting, which
keeps this option open. The highest-warmth play for a prospect worth the minute.

## 8. The commonality finder [AUTO where genuine]
Name a real shared thing (same city, niche, a view they voiced, a place you've been) in
the opener. "Always wanted to visit Utah, seen the drone footage of those mountains."
Wired into the opener draft, but only when the captured context shows a GENUINE
commonality. Never invented. Thin context stays general.

## 9. Address objections, send the link anyway [MANUAL, has skill support]
Objections mean they are still HOT, not lost. Answer the objection and send the call link
regardless; a surprising share still book. The objection-handler skill + objection bank
already hold the counters. Worth wiring counter-drafts into DM follow-ups later.

## 10. Multiple touch points (5+ before a call) [AUTO + MANUAL]
A prospect books after ~5 touches, so never rely on the DM alone. [AUTO] The system
already stacks connect + like + comment on their posts + the day-2 conveyor DM. [MANUAL]
Endorse them, follow up over weeks, send a free training they need. The sequence is the
close, not any single message.
